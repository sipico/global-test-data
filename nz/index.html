🇳🇿 New Zealand Aotearoa New Zealand

New Zealand has no official fictitious NZBN: the two NZBNs in the Peppol PINT A-NZ examples belong to real (removed) companies, and the NZ party data in those examples should not be copied. There is no reserved telephone range, so leave phone numbers out. Inland Revenue publishes the IRD and bank account algorithms with worked examples, but every valid IRD number may belong to a real taxpayer, so none is listed as usable. NHI numbers starting with Z are reserved for testing. NZ Post's examples put invented people at real places; no open postcode file exists to check against.

Valid range 10,000,000-200,000,000 (raised from 150,000,000 on 1 April 2026); mod-11 with weights 3 2 7 6 5 4 3 2, secondary weights 7 4 3 2 5 2 7 6 if the result is 10.
