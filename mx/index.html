🇲🇽 Mexico México

Mexico is the best case found so far: the tax authority SAT publishes 30 official test taxpayers (15 companies, 15 individuals) with test certificates, invented names and RFCs that pass the check digit. They are accepted only by the SAT test environment and PAC sandboxes, and the certificates expire January–May 2027. The company postcodes exist only in the SAT CFDI catalogue (no real settlement). Banxico publishes an example CLABE. No reserved telephone range and no official fictitious street exist. The generic RFCs XAXX010101000 / XEXX010101000 have a legal meaning and are not test values. CFDI is not UBL; the UBL hints are for mapping only. RFC ownership cannot be checked (no open RFC lookup), and the CURP check rule for names with Ñ is unverified.

Generic RFC for the general public (anonymous domestic buyer)
