🇭🇰 Hong Kong 香港

Hong Kong reserves no identifiers or telephone numbers for fiction. There is no official valid Business Registration number: the Inland Revenue Department masks BR numbers in its specimen certificates (5XXXXXXX-000-04-17-A), and the BRN has no published check digit. All eight Immigration Department specimen ID cards deliberately fail the HKID check character, which makes them official, obviously fake values. The closest thing to a safe phone number is the unassigned 74–79 level, which OFCA holds for a future longer numbering plan. Hong Kong has no postcodes (Hongkong Post's own guidance could not be read; secondary sources say to leave the field blank or use 000 or HKG where a form requires one). example.hk and example.com.hk are unregistered today but not reserved, so prefer .test.

Business Registration number (商業登記號碼, BRN)

No check-digit algorithm is published for the 8-digit BRN; validate with ^\d{8}$ only. Since 2023-12-27 the BRN is also the Unique Business Identifier (replacing the company registration number) and Hong Kong's entity TIN.

8 digits; full certificate number 99999999-&&&-&&-&&-& (branch, month, year, check character)—Inland Revenue Departmentcac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:CompanyIDcac:PartyTaxScheme/cbc:CompanyID
Hong Kong Identity Card number (香港身份證號碼, HKID)

The check algorithm is not published by ImmD (widely used algorithm: letters A=10…Z=35, a single letter padded with 36, weights 9…2, 11 − (sum mod 11), 10 → A, 11 → 0). No validator in tools/checks.py yet.

1–2 letters + 6 digits + check character (0-9 or A) in brackets, e.g. A123456(3)—Immigration Department—
