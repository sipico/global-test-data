🇳🇬 Nigeria

The Central Bank of Nigeria's NUBAN documents contain worked examples of the account number check digit, which are the safest Nigerian values found (the bank codes are real). The national e-invoicing system (NRS, formerly FIRS) calls itself UBL- and Peppol-based, but its API takes a JSON derived from UBL, not UBL XML. Its samples use real or invalid data in many places; the TINs in its field documentation (e.g. 89487982-0001) and the generic names (ABC Cement Ltd …) were not checked against the registers and are left out. No reserved phone range, test NIN/BVN or fictitious postcode exists; do not invent Nigerian phone numbers.
