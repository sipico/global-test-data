🇪🇬 Egypt مصر

The SWIFT IBAN Registry gives an official example Egyptian IBAN. The Egyptian Tax Authority's e-invoicing system uses its own JSON/XML format (not UBL); its SDK and Postman collection contain generic names and an EGS item code. The taxpayer registration numbers in those samples (e.g. 674859545) are probably pre-production test taxpayers but could not be verified (no open lookup) and are left out. No reserved phone range, national ID specimen or fictitious postcode exists; the national ID check digit algorithm is not published. Do not invent Egyptian phone numbers.
