🇨🇱 Chile

The Chilean tax authority SII defines generic RUTs with a real meaning: 66.666.666-6 for an anonymous boleta customer and 55.555.555-5 for a foreign receiver without a RUT. Its certification manual uses the "fictitious company" 97975000-5 "RUT DE PRUEBA". None of these can be checked against the register (the SII lookup needs a captcha). Many other SII example RUTs fall in ranges assigned to real people and companies. E-invoices are SII DTE XML, not UBL. Subtel reserves no telephone numbers for fiction, and no official example bank account exists.

Mod 11 with weights 2–7 repeating from the right; 11 → 0, 10 → K. Natural persons roughly ≤ 30 million, legal entities ≈ 50–99 million (approximate, not officially verified).
