What the statuses mean

  • Reserved An authority formally reserves this value or range for fiction, examples or tests.
  • Official example Published by an authority in its own examples. The Checks column shows whether it passes validation.
  • Derived Built from the official format rules and checked to be unassigned.
  • Avoid Circulates as an example but belongs to a real entity or fails validation.

Only values that are reserved, official examples or derived-and-checked are offered for use. Unverified findings stay in the research notes.
