🇨🇳 China (mainland) 中国大陆

Mainland China reserves no identifiers, telephone numbers or postcodes for fiction or tests. The national standards publish worked examples instead: the Unified Social Credit Code example 91350100M000100Y43 from GB 32100-2015 (its existence could not be checked, because the national register needs a captcha) and two resident ID numbers from GB 11643-1999. Deriving more USCCs is easy, but without a register lookup they may belong to real entities, so none are listed. No fictitious bank (CNAPS) code, customs party code or postcode was found; China Post's 999077/999078/999079 are internal sorting codes for Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan. For GLN/GTIN use the GS1 prefix 952 (see the global file); GS1 China prefixes 690–699 are real.
