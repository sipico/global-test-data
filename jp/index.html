🇯🇵 Japan 日本

Japan has an official UBL source, the JP PINT example invoices published by the Digital Agency through OpenPeppol, but their Corporate Numbers and invoice registration numbers (T-numbers) fail the NTA check digit, apparently on purpose, as does the NTA's own T1234567890123. The usable numbers here are those values with the check digit corrected, plus a 999900 series for larger party sets, all confirmed absent from both NTA registers. JPRS reserves example.jp, example.co.jp and example.ne.jp for examples. No phone numbers are reserved for fiction; unassigned MIC blocks are listed instead and may be allocated later. Not included because unverified: the check digit for sole-trader T-numbers, and Tokyo 03-1xxx/2xxx/7xxx-9xxx numbers (not in MIC's list, but the rule was not confirmed). The My Number (individual number) must not be used in samples.

Corporate Number (法人番号)

Check digit = 9 − (Σ Pₙ × Qₙ mod 9), Pₙ = n-th base digit from the right, Qₙ = 1 (odd n) or 2 (even n). Bases starting 0000 belong to government bodies. The NTA publication site lists every number ever assigned, closed entities included.

13 digits; check digit first, then a 12-digit basejp-corporateNational Tax Agency (国税庁)cbc:EndpointID @schemeID=0188cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:CompanyID @schemeID=0188
Qualified Invoice Issuer registration number (適格請求書発行事業者登録番号)

Sole traders get T + 13 digits that are neither a My Number nor a Corporate Number; whether the same check digit applies to these is unverified.
