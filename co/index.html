🇨🇴 Colombia

Colombia's electronic invoice is UBL 2.1. The DIAN toolkit ships 44 signed example XMLs that are an excellent structural reference, but most party NITs in them are real companies, including DIAN's own NIT. Only a handful of DIAN example NITs pass the check digit and are not in the RUES chamber-of-commerce register; they were not checked in the DIAN RUT (captcha), and RUES does not cover every taxpayer. The anonymous consumer has a reserved ID (222222222222). There is no reserved telephone range, no official fictitious address and no national bank-account check digit. Not included: the exógena seudo-NITs 444444xxx (source article not found) and the cédula numbers in the DIAN examples (Registraduría not checked).

NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria)

DV: weights from the right 3 7 13 17 19 23 29 37 41 43 47 53 59 67 71; r = sum mod 11; DV = r if r is 0 or 1, else 11 − r. In the DIAN UBL 2.1 e-invoice the NIT goes without the DV, with schemeName = identification type (31 = NIT), schemeID = DV and schemeAgencyID = 195 (DIAN).

9 digits for legal entities (blocks 800…, 830…, 860…, 890…, 900…, 901…) + 1 check digit (DV); individuals use their cédula as NITco-nitDIANcac:PartyTaxScheme/cbc:CompanyID @schemeName=31 @schemeID=<DV> @schemeAgencyID=195cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:CompanyID @schemeName=31 @schemeID=<DV> @schemeAgencyID=195

Cannot be validated offline; do not invent one. DIAN identification type 13.
