🇹🇼 Taiwan 臺灣

Taiwan reserves no identifiers or telephone numbers for fiction, but the Ministry of Finance's worked examples for the 2023 Unified Business Number check rule give two valid UBNs that are in none of the three official registers. Several obvious placeholder UBNs (00000000, 99999997 …) and MOF's own first example belong to real businesses. The Ministry of the Interior's specimen ID card number deliberately fails the check digit, while the textbook A123456789 passes and is reportedly a real person's number. The MOF e-invoice test platform (wwwtest.einvoice.nat.gov.tw) issues test invoice tracks, but its official test UBNs could not be read (HTTP 403). No unused postcodes could be checked (the 3+3 file needs registration).

IdentifierFormatValidatorAuthorityUBL
Unified Business Number (營利事業統一編號, UBN, 統編)

Weights 1 2 1 2 1 2 4 1; add the digits of each product. Since 2023-04-01 the sum must be divisible by 5 (previously 10); if the 7th digit is 7, sum or sum + 1 may be used. The old rule is a subset of the new one.

National ID (國民身分證統一編號)

Letter value A=10 … (I=34, O=35, W=32, X=30, Y=31, Z=33) split into two digits, weights 1 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2 1 1, total divisible by 10 (check rule from secondary sources). No validator in tools/checks.py yet.
