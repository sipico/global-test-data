🇧🇷 Brazil Brasil

Brazil has strong official material: Receita Federal's worked example for the new alphanumeric CNPJ (12.ABC.345/01DE-35) passes the check digits and is not in the register, Serpro publishes 22 "CPF fictícios" for its API trial, and the NF-e homologação environment reserves a CNPJ (99.999.999/0001-91) and mandatory test texts. Serpro's numeric "fictitious" CNPJs, however, belong to real companies. Brazil does not use UBL or Peppol (national NF-e/CT-e/NFS-e XML). Anatel reserves no telephone numbers for fiction, so leave phones out. State IE (Inscrição Estadual) examples from SINTEGRA were not checked against state registers and are not listed.

CNPJ (Cadastro Nacional da Pessoa Jurídica)

Mod 11 (weights 5-4-3-2-9-8-7-6-5-4-3-2, then 6-5-4-3-2-9-8-7-6-5-4-3-2; remainder < 2 → 0). From July 2026 the first 12 positions may be A–Z (value = ASCII − 48), IN RFB 2.229/2024; numeric CNPJs stay valid.

14 characters, 8-character root + 4-character branch (0001 = head office) + 2 numeric check digits; written NN.NNN.NNN/NNNN-NNbr-cnpjReceita Federal do Brasil—
CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas)

The 9th digit is the fiscal region. There is no open CPF register lookup.

11 digits, 9 base digits + 2 mod-11 check digits; written NNN.NNN.NNN-NNbr-cpfReceita Federal do Brasil—
Inscrição Estadual (IE)

Empty for non-contributors (indIEDest = 9); the literal ISENTO is used for IE-exempt contributors.

Varies by state (length and check-digit routine published on SINTEGRA)—State tax administrations (SEFAZ)—
CEP (postcode)NNNNN-NNN, alone on the last address line, without the word "CEP" and without dots—Correios—
