🇫🇷 France

France has the best official source of fictitious company identifiers found so far: the FNFE-MPE example invoices for the national e-invoicing reform use SIRENs that pass the check digit and are not in the company register. The telecoms regulator ARCEP reserves six phone number blocks for fiction. There are no fictitious towns or postcodes: La Poste's own examples put invented people and companies at real communes.
