🇿🇦 South Africa Suid-Afrika

SARS publishes worked examples that pass its own modulus-10 (Luhn) check digit: an income tax reference, a PAYE/SDL/UIF reference and an ID number. ICASA reserves the geographic exchange codes 114 to 117 for network testing in every area code (test numbers, not a drama range). There is no official example VAT number or company registration number, and no fictitious town or postcode; the UPU addressing examples use real towns. Luhn-valid VAT numbers starting with 4 (e.g. 4000000002) were not checked against the SARS register (interactive lookup only) and are left out. South Africa does not use IBAN, and no e-invoicing standard has been chosen yet.
