🇸🇬 Singapore Singapore / 新加坡 / Singapura / சிங்கப்பூர்

Singapore publishes official UBL 2.1 examples (PINT-SG, the InvoiceNow profile of Peppol) and 19 Myinfo sandbox NRIC/FINs. However, every UEN in IMDA's examples fails its check letter, and some UENs in them, or their "corrected" forms, belong to real entities. The usable UENs here are therefore derived: they pass the check letter and were looked up in ACRA's full open register (2.1 million rows). Local company numbers use a past year whose serial range is closed, so they can never be issued. No authority reserves phone numbers, domains, postcodes or company numbers for fiction. Not obtained: the IRAS Check GST Register sandbox test data and the Myinfo v5 test personas. Postal sector 74 looks unused but was not exhaustively verified.

UEN (Unique Entity Number)

ACRA does not publish the check-letter algorithms; the python-stdnum rules were verified against all 2.1 M ACRA records. Peppol endpoint value is SGUEN + UEN.

Business (ROB) nnnnnnnnX; local company (ROC) yyyynnnnnX; others [TSR]yyPQnnnnXsg-uenACRA (and other issuing agencies for the "others" format)cbc:EndpointID @schemeID=0195cac:PartyLegalEntity/cbc:CompanyID @schemeID=0195
GST registration number

No public check algorithm for the M… format.

Usually the UEN; otherwise M + check character + 7 digits + check letter (10 characters, e.g. M2-1234567-K)—IRAScac:PartyTaxScheme/cbc:CompanyID
NRIC / FIN

ICA does not publish the rule. The M series (FINs from 2022) uses a different table that was not verified.

S/T (citizens, PRs) or F/G/M (foreigners) + 7 digits + check lettersg-nricICA—
