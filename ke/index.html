🇰🇪 Kenya

The Kenya Revenue Authority uses the obviously fictitious PINs A123456789Z and P123456789Z throughout its eTIMS (OSCU/VSCU) specifications; the PIN check letter algorithm is not published, so only the format can be validated. The Communications Authority reserves the national destination code 199 for network testing, but libphonenumber rejects those numbers. eTIMS is a KRA-specific JSON API, not UBL. There is no fictitious town or postcode, no IBAN and no published bank account check digit. The M-Pesa test shortcode 174379 is widely documented but was not confirmed at the source, and the eTIMS customer name MTEJA LIMITED was not checked against the business register; both are left out.
