🇰🇷 South Korea 대한민국

South Korea has no officially reserved example values of any kind: no identifiers, phone numbers or example domains (example.kr and example.co.kr are registered). Its e-tax invoice uses the KEC XML standard, not UBL, and Korea has no Peppol authority. The common "example" business registration number 123-45-67890 fails the check digit. A checksum-valid party-set series (000-81-0nnnC) was derived, but Korean registers could not be queried, so it is not listed as usable. Also not listed because unverified: postcodes 00xxx and 64xxx-99xxx (outside the range Korea Post says it assigned, but not checked against the postcode database) and mobile numbers 010-0xxx/010-1xxx (reportedly not assignable; not confirmed in the MSIT rules). What remains usable are the UPU address examples, the placeholder name 홍길동 and RFC 2606/6761 domains.
